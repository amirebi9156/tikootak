# Product

## Register
product (Operate mode: people complete tasks, chat, call, listen, scroll)

## Users
Iranian friends first, then strangers who join by invite. Persian-first, RTL, often on mid-range Android phones over slow or unstable mobile networks, sometimes on desktop. Used daily, many times a day.

## Product purpose
Private social platform: chat, group calls, reels/feed, stories, shared music playlists. Self-hosted, must be easy to move between servers. It should feel as fast and familiar as Telegram, with the Instagram/music content layer on top.

## Brand personality
Calm, friendly, quick, a little premium. Not corporate, not loud, not template-looking.

## Anti-references
Generic AI-made look (purple gradients, nested cards, bounce animations, identical card grids). Muddy dark blur, white smudges at glass pill edges, tails/halos around bubbles (the owner rejected these).

## Strategic principles
- Familiar beats novel: Telegram layout and behavior for chat; no surprises in navigation.
- Motion is short and purposeful; frequent actions (typing, sending, scrolling) are never slowed by animation.
- Persian typography and RTL are first-class, not an afterthought; mixed Persian/English text must never break layouts.
- Every screen works with the keyboard open, on a 360 px wide phone, offline, and with very long names.
